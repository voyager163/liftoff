# Code of Conduct

Liftoff contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and technical decisions in good faith.
- Give actionable feedback and assume reasonable intent.
- Respect differing backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Keep public communication focused on the project and its community.
- Protect private or sensitive information shared during security and conduct reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, repeated disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close interactions, restrict
participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.
Decisions take context, severity, and repeated behavior into account.

## Report a conduct concern

Send sensitive conduct reports privately to
[ask.msncontrol@gmail.com](mailto:ask.msncontrol@gmail.com). The repository owner has
approved this working, monitored mailbox for **private conduct reports only**.
Do not post sensitive reports in public issues or pull requests.

Include relevant context and links, and share only information needed to
understand the concern. Do not include credentials, unrelated private source or
data, `.env` files, or unredacted diagnostics.

This mailbox is not a support or security-reporting channel:

- Usage questions, bugs, and feature requests use the guides and existing
  GitHub Issues in the [support and reporting map](CONTRIBUTING.md#support-and-reporting).
- Suspected vulnerabilities or secret exposure use the separate
  [private security process](SECURITY.md#report-a-vulnerability), not this mailbox.

## Handling and limits

Liftoff's sole maintainer monitors the mailbox and handles reports on a
best-effort basis. There is no independent response team or guaranteed response
time or outcome. The project cannot offer independent adjudication of a concern
involving the maintainer.

Reports are handled as privately as practical, but confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed. Relevant information may need to be shared to assess a report or
take action; avoid sending sensitive material beyond what is necessary.
