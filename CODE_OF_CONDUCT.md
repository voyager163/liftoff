# Code of Conduct

Liftoff contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and technical decisions in good faith.
- Give actionable feedback and assume reasonable intent.
- Respect differing backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Keep public communication focused on the project and its community.
- Protect private or sensitive information shared during security and conduct reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, repeated disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other community spaces, and when someone represents Liftoff publicly. They
apply to maintainers as well as contributors.

## Reporting and enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[ask.msncontrol@gmail.com](mailto:ask.msncontrol@gmail.com). This address is
monitored by the project maintainer and is separate from vulnerability
reporting. Include a description, relevant public links, whether the behavior
is ongoing, and how you prefer to be contacted. Share only the personal
information needed to understand the report.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. The maintainer will
consider context, severity, impact, and repeated behavior when deciding an
appropriate response, which may range from a warning to removal from project
spaces. Do not retaliate against someone for making a good-faith report.

Liftoff has one maintainer, so it cannot offer an independent enforcement panel
or guarantee a response time. If the concern involves the maintainer, this
limitation still applies. Violations of GitHub's platform policies can also be
[reported directly to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
That route is not independent mediation of project decisions.

Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities and
[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for public technical questions.
